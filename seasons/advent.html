Advent

Advent is a season of waiting and hope, when we prepare our hearts for the coming of Christ, both at Christmas and in our lives today.

Advent Season Intro

Advent comes from the Latin word adventus, meaning “coming” or “arrival.”

It is a time to celebrate Jesus Christ, who came into the world at Bethlehem, who continues to be with us through his Spirit today, and who will come again in glory to complete the work of healing and restoration that began with his birth.

Advent in 2026 begins on Sunday, November 29 and ends on Thursday, December 24.
